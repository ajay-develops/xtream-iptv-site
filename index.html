Privacy Policy — Xtream IPTV

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Xtream IPTV ("the app") is published by Saul Redemption Services LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

The app works without sending any personal information to us. Everything it saves stays on your device. If you choose to turn on Diagnostics (Settings > Diagnostics, off by default), the app also sends anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics, described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Source details: the M3U URLs, Xtream server addresses, usernames and passwords you enter. These are encrypted using the Android Keystore.
  • Catalog: lists of channels, movies and series downloaded from your sources, kept in the app's private storage (not accessible to other apps).
  • Favorites and watch history: used for Continue watching and resume playback. History is encrypted and can be cleared in Settings.
  • Cached artwork: images supplied by your sources, cached temporarily.

This data never leaves your device except as described below. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Optional diagnostics (off by default)

If you turn on Settings > Diagnostics > Share crash reports and usage statistics, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to send us:

  • Crash reports: the technical details of a crash, such as the error and stack trace, app version, device model, Android version, and the device's state at the time (for example free memory).
  • Usage statistics: a few anonymous events, such as that a source was added (M3U or Xtream), that a catalog update finished or failed (with rough size and duration ranges and an error type), that playback started or failed (with an error code), and whether the app is running on a phone or a TV.
  • A random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase to count devices. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account.

These reports never include your playlist links, server addresses, usernames, passwords, playlist names, channel or movie titles, what you watch, or your advertising ID. The app does not request the advertising ID.

We use this information only to find and fix crashes, improve reliability, and understand which features are used. Google processes it for us as a service provider under Google's privacy terms for Firebase. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days and analytics events for up to 2 months.

You can turn Diagnostics off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops all collection and deletes analytics data stored on your device. To ask us to delete data already received, email us at the address below.

Network connections

The app connects to the servers you configure (your IPTV provider, playlist, guide, and the artwork and stream addresses they supply). Those servers are operated by third parties, and their handling of your data is governed by their own policies. Your device's IP address and credentials are sent to them as required to load your content. Some providers use unencrypted HTTP; the app warns you when a source does not use HTTPS. If you turn on Diagnostics, the app also connects to Google's Firebase services.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising SDKs, and no advertising ID
  • No account or registration
  • No sale of data, and no sharing with third parties other than Google as described above
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files

Permissions

The app requests Internet access, network state (to detect connectivity), and wake lock (to keep the screen on during playback).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Content

The app is a player and does not provide or host any content. You are responsible for having the right to access the sources you add.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
