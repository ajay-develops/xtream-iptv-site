Privacy Policy — Xtream IPTV

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Xtream IPTV ("the app") is published by Saul Redemption Services LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or share your personal information on our servers. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Source details: the M3U URLs, Xtream server addresses, usernames and passwords you enter. These are encrypted using the Android Keystore.
  • Catalog snapshots: lists of channels, movies and series downloaded from your sources.
  • Favorites and watch history: used for Continue watching and resume playback. History is encrypted and can be cleared in Settings.
  • Cached artwork: images supplied by your sources, cached temporarily.

This data never leaves your device except as described below. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network connections

The app connects only to the servers you configure (your IPTV provider, playlist, guide, and the artwork and stream addresses they supply). Those servers are operated by third parties, and their handling of your data is governed by their own policies. Your device's IP address and credentials are sent to them as required to load your content. Some providers use unencrypted HTTP; the app warns you when a source does not use HTTPS.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No account or registration
  • No sale or sharing of data
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files

Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Content

The app is a player and does not provide or host any content. You are responsible for having the right to access the sources you add.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
